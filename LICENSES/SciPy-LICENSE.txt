picasso/zfit.py: _minimize_z_device is a port of _minimize_scalar_bounded
of SciPy (https://github.com/scipy/scipy, scipy/optimize/_optimize.py,
Brent's method as used by scipy.optimize.minimize_scalar with bounds) to
numba CUDA, so that the GPU z fit gives the result of the CPU fit. It
remains a derivative work of SciPy's source, so SciPy's license is
reproduced below in full.
