Picasso's spline PSF calibration computes its cubic-spline coefficients with
an implementation derived from Gpuspline (https://github.com/gpufit/Gpuspline).
Its coefficient scheme - a tensor product of 1D natural cubic interpolating
splines - and the flat coefficient-buffer layout Picasso's fitters read were
reimplemented in Python/NumPy/SciPy in

    picasso/spline.py                  spline_coefficients()

Picasso no longer links against or redistributes the Gpuspline binary, but that
implementation follows its scheme and layout, so its license is reproduced
below in full.
