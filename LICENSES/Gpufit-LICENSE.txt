Picasso's GPU and CPU curve fitters are derived from Gpufit
(https://github.com/gpufit/Gpufit). Its Levenberg-Marquardt driver, its
Gauss-Jordan solve, its least-squares and Poisson maximum-likelihood estimators
and its Gaussian and cubic-spline PSF models were ported to Python/numba in

    picasso/fitting/splinefit.py       CPU cubic-spline fitting
    picasso/fitting/splinefit_cuda.py  CUDA cubic-spline fitting
    picasso/fitting/gaussfit_cuda.py   CUDA Gaussian fitting
    picasso/fitting/lmfit_cuda.py      shared CUDA Levenberg-Marquardt
                                       machinery

Picasso no longer links against or redistributes the Gpufit binary, but those
modules remain derivative works of its source, so its license is reproduced
below in full.
