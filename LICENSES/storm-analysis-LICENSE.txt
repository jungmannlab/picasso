Picasso's multi-emitter fitting is a port of the 3D-DAOSTORM analysis of
storm-analysis (https://github.com/ZhuangLab/storm-analysis, v2.4.1). Its peak
finder, its per-peak Levenberg-Marquardt fitter and its peak pruning were
ported to Python/numba in

    picasso/fitting/residualfit.py     multi-emitter fitting of whole frames

from the storm-analysis files sa_library/fitting.py, sa_library/multi_fit.c,
sa_library/dao_fit.c, sa_library/ia_utilities.c and daostorm_3d/find_peaks.py,
each of which carries the license below. Picasso does not link against or
redistribute storm-analysis, but that module is a derivative work of its
source, so the license is reproduced below in full.

Cite: Babcock, H., Sigal, Y. M. & Zhuang, X. "A high-density 3D localization
algorithm for stochastic optical reconstruction microscopy." Optical Nanoscopy
1, 6 (2012). https://doi.org/10.1186/2192-2853-1-6
