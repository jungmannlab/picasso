Contributing
============

Feature requests and bug reports are welcome on the `GitHub issue tracker
<https://github.com/jungmannlab/picasso/issues>`__, and so are pull requests.
We will gladly guide you through the codebase and credit you accordingly. You
can also contact us at picasso@jungmannlab.org.

To extend Picasso without changing its code, write a plugin, see
:ref:`plugins-for-developers`. For a local installation that picks up your
changes, see the *Developer installation* tab in
:doc:`getting-started/installation`.
